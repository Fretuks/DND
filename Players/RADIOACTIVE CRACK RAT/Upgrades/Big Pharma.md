The cure and stabilizer are medical creations originating from the Hive. The Hive discovered something horrifying: Meridian Rot is not truly a disease. 

It is an incomplete symbiotic organism attempting forced adaptation with incompatible hosts. Most people die because the body rejects integration. The Skaven survived because Skaven biology is unusually resilient and adaptive.

The Hive eventually produced:
- a **Purge Serum**
- a **Stabilization Serum**
Both are experimental.

---
# The Stabilizer
The stabilizer does not remove the symbiote. It completes the adaptation process. The symbiote and host stop fighting. Instead they synchronize.

---
## Stabilized Symbiosis
The organism permanently integrates into the Skaven’s biology.
The disease:
- no longer progresses
- no longer causes degeneration
- no longer risks Stage V death

Both the symbiote and the skaven survive.
## Mechanical Changes
### Removed
- Infection Stage progression
- Terminal degeneration
- Biological strain penalties
### Venom System Upgrade
Venom becomes Symbiotic Secretion. The Skaven no longer “forces” venom production. The symbiote produces it naturally.
### Changes
- Venom Charges become unlimited
- Applying venom no longer costs charges
- Venom duration:
    - 3 hits / 3 rounds
However:
- only one weapon may still be coated at once
### New Passive - Perfected Host
The body has fully adapted.
- Posture Regain +2
- Movement Speed +1.5 meters
- +1 Constitution
- +1 Dexterity
### New Passive - Symbiotic Reflex
The organism reacts before conscious thought.
Once per combat:
- automatically succeed one Dodge Throw
### New Passive - Shared Survival
When reduced to 0 HP:
- immediately regain 25% Posture
- stand back up at 1 HP
The symbiote forcibly restarts bodily function.

---
# The Cure
The cure aggressively kills the symbiote. The process is agonizing because the organism resists removal violently.

The symbiote understands:
- this will kill it
- the host chose this willingly

It panics. 

---
## Cured State
The body finally stabilizes naturally. Without the symbiote:
- organs recover
- breathing improves
- nervous system calms
- muscles function efficiently
### Removed
- Venom Coating
- Infection Stage progression
- Symbiote interactions
### Replaced with Refined Toxin System
The Skaven now uses crafted toxins instead of biological venom
### New Passive - Stabilized Physiology
- Posture Regain +1
- Movement Speed +1.5 meters
- +1 Constitution
- +1 Strength
### New Passive - Controlled Metabolism
- Toxicity thresholds delayed by 1 stage
- Withdrawal penalties reduced:
    - no movement penalty
    - no posture regain penalty
### New Passive - Clean Blood
- Gas AC +10
- Necrotic AC +10
- Automatically succeed Constitution checks against disease
