**Max Momentum:** 10  
Momentum cannot go below 0 or above maximum.

| Momentum | State         | Effect                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------- | ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0        | Broken Flow   | -1 to attack rolls, -3m movement, -2 Dodge, cannot use Burst or Jump                                                                                                              |
| 1-2      | Unstable Flow | -1 to attack rolls, -1 Dodge                                                                                                                                                      |
| 3-5      | Stable Flow   | +3m movement                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 6-8      | High Flow     | +3m movement, +1 Dodge, first Momentum Mantra each round costs 1 less Momentum.                                                                                                   |
| 9        | Critical Flow | +3m movement, +1 Dodge, first Momentum Mantra each round costs 1 less Momentum.<br><br>Gain one extra **mantra-only Bonus Action** this turn                                      |
| 10       | Maximum Flow  | +3m movement, +1 Dodge, first Momentum Mantra each round costs 1 less Momentum.<br><br>Gain one extra **mantra-only Action** and one extra **mantra-only Bonus Action** this turn |
### Maximum Flow
**At 10 Momentum**:
- Extra actions cannot be used for weapon attacks, item use, Dash, Disengage, Hide, grapples, shoves, or module attacks.
- The Mantras still pay all Ether, Momentum, condition, and cooldown costs.
- After using either extra action, immediately lose **3 Momentum**.
- If you use both extra actions in the same turn, lose **5 Momentum** total instead.
- You cannot regain Momentum again until the end of that turn.

### Momentum Gain
- Move 6m or more: +1
- Successful Dodge: +1
- Successful Parry: +1
- Airborne hit: +1
- Kill within 1 round of engagement: +2
Maximum **+3 Momentum per round** from all sources. Momentum gained from Parry/Dodge still requires spending the normal Reaction.

### Momentum Loss
- Do not move at least 3m during your turn: -1
- Become Staggered: -2
- Take damage: -1
- Take Fire or Lightning damage: -2 instead
	- Fire and Lightning damage replace the normal damage Momentum loss; they do not stack with it.
### Momentum Abilities
#### Jump - 1 Momentum
Double high-jump and long-jump distance.
#### Burst - 1 Momentum
Gain +3m movement speed for 1 turn.
- Maximum twice per turn.
- Cannot be used at 3 Momentum or less.
- No action required.
