### 3-D Printer
**Theme:** "I may be out of shells, but I'm not out of spells."

Summon an additional rocket out of thin air. Where did it come from? Your ass? Who knows?

**Activation:** Once per short or long rest.

**Effect:**
- Immediately conjure **1 temporary rocket** of any unlocked rarity.
- This rocket must be fired before the end of combat or it will be lost.
- Temporary rockets cannot be restocked or saved.

**Corrupted Version:**
- Conjure **2 temporary rockets** instead.
- If both rockets hit at least one enemy, regain **2 Ether**.

### Quarterly Returns
**Theme:** “Friendly fire actually isn't friendly at all.”

**Description:**  
After causing enough collateral damage you have been promoted to destruction-specialist.

**Activation:** Once per combat.

**Effect:**

- Activate after a rocket hits **2 or more enemies**.
- Gain **+1 SV token** for each enemy hit after the first.
- Spend SV tokens during the same combat:
    - **1 token:** +5% Penetration on next rocket
    - **2 tokens:** +1.5 meter rocket radius
    - **3 tokens:** +1 damage die on next rocket

**Corrupted Version:**

- You also gain **1 SV token** when allies are damaged by your rockets.
- Spending **3 tokens** also gives the rocket **+2 Posture Damage**.
